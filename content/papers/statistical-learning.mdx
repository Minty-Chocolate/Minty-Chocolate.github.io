## Reading focus

This entry is a sample review of a textbook rather than a single research paper. My focus is the relationship between flexibility, generalization, and the design of a fair comparison.

## A recurring lesson

Regularization expresses a preference for certain solutions. That preference may be more valuable than extra model capacity when the data are limited or unstable.

## Working questions

How sensitive is the conclusion to the split? Were tuning decisions made using information from the evaluation set? Is the baseline strong enough to make the result meaningful?
