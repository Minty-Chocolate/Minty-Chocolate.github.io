## The core idea

The paper presents the Transformer: an architecture built around attention rather than recurrence or convolution for sequence transduction. These are sample reading notes, intended to demonstrate this review format.

## A useful equation

Scaled dot-product attention maps queries, keys, and values to a weighted combination:

$$
\operatorname{Attention}(Q,K,V) = \operatorname{softmax}\left(\frac{QK^\top}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)V.
$$

## My reading

The interesting modeling choice is that relationships are learned directly across positions. Positional information supplies the ordering that attention alone does not encode.
