In the paper **Quantifying the Contributions of Clustering in Statistical Arbitrage**, a plethora of ML clustering algorithms are applied to determine their 
usefulness in statistical arbitrage. The setup is all the same except the clustering module in the pipeline. All other configurations including signal and 
portfolio construction are kept minimal and simple to isolate the effect. It is a clean and somewhat simplistic experiment to compare clustering algorithms 
applied to financial markets. From the results presented in the paper, it seemed that K-means is the clear winner. 

I tested the algorithm from Algorithms for Learning Graphs in Financial Markets, which is a promising graph clustering algorithm specifically tailored for 
financial data and the results surpassed that of K-means, with higher returns, lower volatility and better monotonicity.
