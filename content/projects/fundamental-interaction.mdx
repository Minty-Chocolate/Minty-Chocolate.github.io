The paper **Can AI Do Financial Research? LLM-Guided Hypothesis Discovery in Asset Pricing** used LLM to mine 11 significant interaction signals from fundementals 
of different themes. One caveat that the data comes from annual Compustat data, which is backward looking in nature, hence the signals ares naturally staler and weaker. 
But if they do represent genuine mispricing and are economically significant, a valuable direction to take would be to apply these to forward looking information 
and see if the same patterns emerge (e.g. analyst predictions, company guidances).

I tested the 11 signals on PIT large cap stocks. The results are mixed, some of them are actually robust and may reflect genuine mispricing. It serves as an potential
reminder that, besides industry KPIs and earnings predictions, there are other ways to do fundamental research. And it's important to understand the economic 
inituition behind the accounting formulas.
